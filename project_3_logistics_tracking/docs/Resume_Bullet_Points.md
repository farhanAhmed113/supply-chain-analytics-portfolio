### Option A: Performance & Cost Optimization Focused

* **Logistics & Freight Shipment Tracking Intelligence Dashboard | SQL, Power BI, Excel**
  * Evaluated freight transportation performance across 4,000+ dispatches and 12 interstate transit corridors, managing $10.8M in multi-modal freight expenditure (Air, Road FTL/LTL, Ocean/Rail).
  * Monitored contracted carrier SLA compliance (On-Time Delivery), identifying an average 4.2% SLA deficit among long-haul carriers and pinpointing transit bottlenecks across high-volume corridors.
  * Executed a Root-Cause Pareto analysis over 500+ shipment delay incidents in SQL, revealing 52% of delays stemmed from port congestion and weather bottlenecks rather than carrier transit failures.
  * Designed an interactive Power BI logistics dashboard tracking Cost-per-Ton-Mile and lane profitability, identifying $1.4M in expedited freight premiums to inform network re-routing strategies.
