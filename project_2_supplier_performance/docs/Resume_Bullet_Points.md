# Resume Bullet Points: Project 2 - Supplier Performance & Procurement Scorecard

Copy and adapt these bullet points for your resume:

---

### Option A: Quantified & Business Impact Focused

* **Supplier Performance & Strategic Procurement Scorecard | SQL, Power BI, Excel**
  * Built an automated vendor scorecard analyzing 2,000+ purchase orders across 15 global suppliers managing $32M+ in annual procurement expenditure.
  * Formulated and tracked end-to-end fulfillment KPIs including On-Time In-Full (OTIF), revealing an 8.2% gap between nominal on-time delivery (89%) and actual fulfillment (82%) caused by short shipments.
  * Developed a Purchase Price Variance (PPV) tracking pipeline in SQL, identifying $684K in off-contract price discrepancies and providing actionable leverage for annual contract renegotiations.
  * Created an executive Power BI dashboard with a dynamic 4-pillar vendor rating index (OTIF, Defect Rate, PPV, Lead Time), segmenting suppliers into Strategic, Preferred, and Watchlist tiers.

### Option B: Technical & Analytics Process Focused

* **Procurement Data Modeling & Vendor Intelligence System | SQL, Power BI, DAX**
  * Engineered a star-schema procurement data warehouse integrating supplier master dimensions, component catalog data, and purchase order fulfillment facts.
  * Programmed complex DAX measures and CTE-based SQL queries to automate quality rejection rate tracking across 1.2M delivered components, maintaining an average 98.4% quality acceptance baseline.
  * Enabled cross-functional sourcing decision-making by creating interactive scatter plots and geographic spend maps in Power BI for Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs).
