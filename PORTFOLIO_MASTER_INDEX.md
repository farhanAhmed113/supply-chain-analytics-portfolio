## 🎯 The Software ➡️ Supply Chain Transition Narrative

When an interviewer asks: **"Why are you transitioning from software to supply chain?"**

Use this exact 3-part framework:
1. **The Technical Foundation**: *"In software, I developed strong capabilities in relational databases, data pipelines, and logic design."*
2. **The Business Passion**: *"However, I discovered that I am energized by physical operational problems—optimizing warehouse working capital, tracking supplier lead times, and preventing transportation bottlenecks."*
3. **The Unfair Advantage**: *"Supply chain teams often struggle with data silos and manual spreadsheet reporting. My background means I can bridge the gap immediately: I write production SQL queries, build clean star-schema BI models, and translate messy operational telemetry into clear executive decisions."*
